Make a Calaf deck called "LBO basics" from these, one card per question — six cards is right:

1. What is a leveraged buyout?
2. Why does leverage increase equity returns?
3. What makes a good LBO candidate?
4. What are the main sources of returns in an LBO?
5. What is a dividend recap?
6. How do you calculate IRR roughly from a money multiple and a holding period?
